The one problem

A brand new session shows a mostly empty chart

These two sit in the same file, 250 lines apart. Read them together:

insightsWindow.ts:271 — the promise
 * The span is what has **elapsed**, not the five hours: the remaining part of
 * the window holds nothing by construction, and drawing it would put empty
 * buckets on the right of every timeline — read as a fleet that stopped.
insightsWindow.ts:86 and :336 — what actually happens
const SESSION_MIN_SPAN_MS = 30 * MINUTE_MS;
const bucketMs = Math.ceil(Math.max(spanMs, SESSION_MIN_SPAN_MS) / 20);
buckets: SESSION_BUCKETS,   // always 20 — so the chart is always ≥ 30 min wide

A window that reset 4 minutes ago has 4 minutes of history. The chart draws 30 minutes of it. 26 of those minutes are in the future — exactly the empty bars on the right the first comment says this design avoids.

For the first half hour after a reset — which is exactly when someone who just got rate-limited comes looking — up to 85% of the chart is empty.

Time since reset Bar width Chart runs past now by Empty bars

Probably not a code bug. The 30-minute rule may well be right and the comment just overstated. But they can’t both stand: either the comment admits the exception, or the chart stops at now. That’s the author’s call, which is why this is flagged rather than guessed at.

Numbers worked out by copying the two functions and running them. The repo’s dependencies aren’t installed in this environment, so that’s the maths, not the real module.
