It moves out of the right-hand rail and sits full-width above the line, because it is the one thing on this page that is usually waiting on you.

What one PR costs, before and after

same four gates, same verdicts

Now · a silo tower each

184px of tower + 86px of meta beside it, per PR, in a 360px rail. Seven open PRs is a column you scroll. The rocket on a full silo is the word this change is trying to free.

After · a row each

#142 Fix the flaky reap test Your call

36px, and the ladder is the same siloGates fill read sideways. Seven PRs is 250px — the whole open set at a glance, which is the thing the towers could never give.

The five rules the drawing rests on

what to preserve

Court decides the order, not fill

The current sort is fullest-first, so a PR you must decide on sits below three the harness is already fixing. Order is attention.status — read off the server, never re-derived, exactly as siloCourt already does it. Fill only breaks ties inside a group.

Red is spent on your court, not on a failed gate

An unmet gate goes unlit, not red. The floor rations red for “a question only you can answer”, and that is the court chip. Today .fx-gates li.no spends it four times per PR, which is why nothing on the panel stands out.

The rocket leaves the PR entirely

Icon pr here; rocket stays on the Goal Floor’s launch stage. In iconForEventKind, pr_merged drops to pr and issue_closed gains rocket — it currently falls through to a flask, so the one real launch is the one event with no rocket on it.

Reasons are quoted, never parsed

The why column is health.reasons joined, or the rejection note for a settled PR. It supplies no count and no colour — the ladder's denominator stays the fixed four of siloGates.

Empty still draws

No open PRs draws the strip with one line — nothing on the rack — never nothing at all. Same rule as the fault gauge: a surface that vanishes when quiet is indistinguishable from a surface that broke.

The Yard gives up its PR list

This strip is the only detailed PR surface, so the Yard becomes issues and stories, and carries the -ignore toggle over. That is the simplification: one subject, one place, which is the same argument that dissolved the act rail into gauges.
